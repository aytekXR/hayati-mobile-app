## How pairing works

When you invite your partner, ikimiz creates an invite link with an eight-character code. Tapping that link opens your browser and shows you the code. It does not open the app directly.

To pair:

1. Install ikimiz first
2. Open the link your partner sent you
3. Copy the code from the page
4. Open ikimiz and tap "Have a code?"
5. Enter the code

Invite codes expire 48 hours after they are created.

## The code does not work

If the code is not accepted:

- Check that the code is exactly eight characters
- Check that fewer than 48 hours have passed since it was created
- Ask your partner to create a fresh invite

## How do I download my data?

In Settings, tap "Download my data". The app shows your data as a document you can copy anywhere you like. Nothing is sent by email. The export contains only your own data and never includes what your partner wrote.

## How do I delete my account?

In Settings, tap "Delete account and data". This permanently removes:

- Your account
- Your private reflections
- The entire shared space with your partner, both sides of every answer

This cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage that in your App Store settings.

Your partner will see, calmly and inside the app, that the shared space has closed. They will not see why or who closed it.

## How do I lock the app?

In Settings you can set a 6-digit PIN. You can also enable Face ID or Touch ID as a shortcut once the PIN is set.

This is local protection. It blocks casual access to the app but is not forensic-grade. Someone with your device passcode or Apple ID can force entry through account recovery, which signs you out and removes the PIN.

## How do I hide the app icon?

In Settings you can choose a discreet icon. This changes the icon image on your home screen. The app name still appears under it. Only the image changes, not the name.

## Is the AI coach therapy?

No. The coach is a gentle companion for reflection and ideas. It is not therapy, not medical or psychological care, and nothing it says is professional advice. If something in your life feels serious or unsafe, please reach out to a qualified person or professional help. The coach may pause and point you toward that help when a message sounds like a crisis.

## Are coach conversations saved?

No. Coach conversations are held only in your device memory while you use them. They are gone when you close the app or sign out. Nothing is stored on our servers or your device.

## Who can use ikimiz?

ikimiz is for adults 18 or older. It is a companion for couples working on their relationship. It is not a dating service, not a matchmaking service, and not a way to hide an app from someone.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store, not ikimiz. To cancel:

1. Open your iPhone Settings
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap Subscriptions
4. Find ikimiz and tap Cancel

Cancelling stops future renewals. It does not create a refund. Deleting your ikimiz account does not cancel your subscription.
