App icons — four concepts + the discreet alternate

All four are wordless: the trademark is unresolved (ADR-027), so every mark must survive a rename to İkimiz, Baynana, Mawadda, or Roohi. All are drawn symmetric-safe and certified RTL-neutral. No hearts, no flames, no faces — two-ness is always paired forms.

1 · Two Seeds, redrawn Recommended — ship this

Two pomegranate seeds inclining toward each other with a clear asymmetric kiss-point — left seed larger and lower in Pomegranate Deep, right smaller and higher in Pomegranate with a single Moonlight specular crescent. Slim teardrops, 1:1.6, defined tips, mark at ~55% of canvas, tips converging ~58% up. The one sanctioned glow: a radial lift toward Night Raised behind the kiss-point, ≤8% delta. Carries the streak metaphor; reads two-ness without bodies; stays quiet on a glanced-at home screen.

iOS 18: dark variant identical (already dark — lift background to Night Raised #2E2344 only if the system double-darkens); tinted variant is the seed pair as a single Moonlight-alpha glyph on transparent. Light contexts drop the highlight.

2 · The Whole Pomegranate Backup · umbrella mark

A single round fruit at ~60% canvas, five-point calyx crown in Clay breaking the silhouette top-end, a thin crescent cut on the lower right revealing exactly two Moonlight seeds inside. Reads fruit-first, love-second — the most glance-proof romantic option for phones checked by family, and the natural umbrella if packs and quizzes become sub-brands: seeds of the same fruit.

3 · The Unfold A/B challenger

An abstract folded note caught mid-opening: two soft-cornered Night Raised panels with Veil edge hairlines lifting apart from a center fold, the gap glowing Sand from within and falling to Night. The negative space between the panels forms a subtle seed silhouette. Brands the product's core verb — the sealed answer, the mutual reveal — in the "icon = the interaction" school. Fully non-romantic at a glance: its superpower for discretion, its risk on the shelf. Test against Two Seeds in a store-page A/B once analytics exist.

4 · The Lit Lattice v2 · AR-storefront refresh

An eight-point star aperture (Seljuk/mashrabiya-derived) at 50% canvas cut into the Night field, glowing warm Sand to Gold at the very center — the one sanctioned gold use in iconography — with two tiny seed silhouettes at 8% scale just visible within, and the glow bleeding 2–3% past the star edges. A lit window seen from the street at night: a warm private life glimpsed but not exposed. The most premium and ownable, and the most abstract — hold for a v2 refresh or the AR storefront.

5 · Discreet alternate Keep — do not "improve"

The shipped grey utility-list glyph, redrawn for pixel hygiene only. It must never gain seeds, pomegranate tones, or any brand tell — its entire job is to not be this brand. iOS 18 dark/tinted variants stay equally anonymous. The honest settings disclosure stays: "Shows a plain icon on your home screen. The app's name still appears under it."
