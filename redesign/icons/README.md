# Hayati — App Icon Concepts

Production-extracted vector assets from the QA-passed app-icon board. Four brand concepts plus the discreet alternate, each as a self-contained SVG (squircle mask, own defs, 120×120 viewBox). Open [icon-preview.html](./icon-preview.html) for the full board with the 29px small-size legibility row.

**Wordless by design.** No concept carries a wordmark: the "Hayati" trademark is unresolved (ADR-027), so every mark must survive a possible rename to İkimiz, Baynana, Mawadda, or Roohi. All marks are drawn symmetric-safe and certified RTL-neutral — no hearts, no flames, no faces; two-ness is always paired forms.

---

## 1 · Two Seeds, redrawn — RECOMMENDED, ship this

<img src="./two-seeds.svg" width="120" alt="Two Seeds icon, dark default"> <img src="./two-seeds-light.svg" width="120" alt="Two Seeds icon, light context variant">

Two pomegranate seeds inclining toward each other with a clear asymmetric kiss-point — left seed slightly larger and lower in Pomegranate Deep, right slightly smaller and higher in Pomegranate with a single Moonlight specular crescent. It keeps continuity with every shipped asset, carries the streak metaphor (every mutual day, a seed), and reads two-ness without bodies while staying quiet on a glanced-at home screen. The redraw fixes the shipped mark's three actual failures: scale, silhouette ambiguity, and tip definition.

**Why recommended:** it is the only concept that is simultaneously on-brand, continuous with shipped assets, and glance-discreet — the others trade one of those away. The light-context variant (`two-seeds-light.svg`, Paper background, no Moonlight highlight) is for marketing and the web favicon.

## 2 · The Whole Pomegranate — backup / umbrella mark

<img src="./whole-pomegranate.svg" width="120" alt="Whole Pomegranate icon">

A single round fruit with a five-point Clay calyx crown and a thin crescent cut on the lower right revealing exactly two Moonlight seeds inside. Reads as fruit first, love second — the most glance-proof romantic option for phones checked by family. It is also the strongest umbrella mark if packs and quizzes become sub-brands: seeds of the same fruit.

## 3 · The Unfold — A/B challenger

<img src="./the-unfold.svg" width="120" alt="The Unfold icon">

An abstract folded note caught mid-opening: two soft-cornered Night Raised panels lifting apart from a center fold, the gap glowing Sand from within, the negative space forming a subtle seed silhouette. Brands the product's core verb — the sealed answer, the mutual reveal — in the Linear/Stripe school of "icon = the interaction." Fully non-romantic at a glance: its superpower for discretion, its risk on the store shelf; test against Two Seeds in an App Store product-page A/B once analytics exist.

## 4 · The Lit Lattice — v2 / AR-storefront refresh

<img src="./lit-lattice.svg" width="120" alt="Lit Lattice icon">

An eight-point star aperture (Seljuk/mashrabiya-derived) cut into the Night field, glowing warm Sand to Gold at the very center — the one sanctioned gold use in iconography — with two tiny seed silhouettes just visible within. Encodes the deepest brand truth — a warm private life glimpsed but not exposed, a lit window seen from the street at night — with unmistakable Anatolian/Arab fluency and zero Western-couple-app resemblance. The most premium and ownable, but also the most abstract: hold for a v2 refresh or the AR storefront.

## 5 · Discreet alternate — keep, do not "improve"

<img src="./discreet-list-glyph.svg" width="120" alt="Discreet grey list-glyph icon">

The shipped grey utility-list glyph, redrawn for pixel hygiene only. Its purpose is privacy from phone-checking outsiders — the relative or metro-glancer who would otherwise identify the app on the home screen. It must never gain seeds, pomegranate tones, or any brand tell: its entire job is to not be this brand. The honest settings disclosure stays: "Shows a plain icon on your home screen. The app's name still appears under it."
